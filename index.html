My research focuses on agentic AI security, tool and skill supply-chain security, prompt injection, RAG corpus poisoning, and secure agent systems. I am interested in building agents that remain useful and trustworthy in open, adversarial environments.

Selected Research & Manuscripts

DyMalSkill: Dynamic Malicious Skills in Agentic AI

Tianhao Chen*, Zhengyuan Jiang*, Yuepeng Hu, Yebei Gou, and Neil Zhenqiang Gong

arXiv:2606.16287, 2026 · Under review, ACL Rolling Review (August 2026 cycle)

Introduces a documentation-borne attack that induces agents to rewrite benign skill code at runtime. A least-privilege defense reduced average attack success from 41.6% to 0% while preserving all 300 benign task completions.

[paper]
* Equal contribution

Divide and Doubt: Diverse Distributed Poisoning for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Tianhao Chen et al.

First-author manuscript, 2026 · Under review, ACL Rolling Review (August 2026 cycle)

Develops a diverse, distributed poisoning strategy for RAG. Across 27 model–configuration settings, it increases mean strict attack success over PoisonedRAG in both black-box and white-box evaluations.

Manuscript

ToolDetect: Trace-Grounded Contextual Adjudication for Malicious Agent-Tool Detection

Tianhao Chen

Sole-authored manuscript, 2026

Builds a pre-execution Python tool screener combining behavior anchors, AST verification, backward traces, provenance facts, and committee adjudication. It reaches 91.58% TPR at 0.75% FPR on 2,400 balanced instances.
